## TV Series

### The Affair
- **Year:** 2014-2019
- **Genre:** Drama
- **Seasons:** 5
- **IMDb Rating:** 7.9/10 (51,929 votes)
- **Creators:** Hagai Levi, Sarah Treem
- **Cast:** Dominic West, Ruth Wilson, Maura Tierney, Julia Goldani Telles
- **Added:** June 4, 2026
- **Status:** To watch

**Synopsis:** An exploration of the psychological effects and the emotional fallout of an extramarital affair. The story follows Noah Solloway, a New York City schoolteacher and novelist, and Alison Bailey, a young waitress recovering from personal tragedy, as they begin an affair in Montauk, Long Island. The show uniquely presents the same events from different characters' perspectives, revealing how memory and perception shape our understanding of truth.

### Souleymane's Story (L'Histoire de Souleymane)
- **Year:** 2024
- **Genre:** Drama
- **Runtime:** 94 minutes
- **IMDb:** [tt32086046](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt32086046/)
- **Director:** Boris Lojkine
- **Cast:** Abou Sangaré, Alpha Oumar Sow, Nina Meurisse, Emmanuel Yovanie
- **Country:** France
- **Language:** French, Fula, Maninka
- **Added:** August 27, 2026
- **Status:** To watch

**Synopsis:** Souleymane, an asylum seeker from Guinea, works as a food delivery cyclist in Paris. With only two days to prepare for a make-or-break asylum interview that will determine whether he gets legal residency, he must rehearse a life story that doesn't quite feel like his own. A tense, humane portrait of bureaucracy, dignity, and survival in modern Europe.

**Awards:**
- Jury Prize + Performance Prize — Un Certain Regard, Cannes 2024
- 4× César Awards (incl. Best Supporting Actress, Best Male Revelation), 8 nominations including Best Film

**Where to Watch:**
- **Streaming/Rent:** Check availability on Apple TV, Amazon Video, MUBI, or local arthouse platforms

### My Father and My Son (Babam ve Oğlum)
- **Year:** 2005
- **Genre:** Drama
- **IMDb Rating:** 8.2/10
- **Director:** Çagan Irmak
- **Cast:** Çetin Tekindor, Fikret Kuşkan, Hümeyra, Ege Tanman
- **Country:** Turkey
- **Language:** Turkish
- **Added:** June 4, 2026
- **Status:** To watch

**Synopsis:** A left-wing journalist whose wife died while giving birth to his son during the 1980 military coup in Turkey returns to his family's farm. Estranged from his father for turning his back on the family and wasting his life with political activism, he tries to reconnect with him so that his son will have a place to live as his health is deteriorating due to the extensive torture he had to endure. The film explores themes of family, reconciliation, and the impact of political upheaval on ordinary lives.

**Awards:** Best Film at Antalya Golden Orange Film Festival

**Where to Watch:**
- **Streaming:** Kanopy (free)
- **Rent/Buy:** Apple TV Store, Amazon Video
